## What's Available

- **Investor Portal** — Qualified investors can now access production reports, legal documentation, and quarterly updates through the DerivaConnect partner portal
- **Due Diligence Package** — Our standard due diligence package is now available to prospective investors upon NDA execution, covering E&P contract structure, reserve summaries, and financial projections
- **Express Interest Form** — Prospective investors and oil field services partners can now formally express interest through our online form for an introductory call within two business days

## About DerivaConnect

DerivaConnect holds contractually secured agreements to conduct oil exploration and production in Venezuela, combined with fully integrated oil field services operations. We offer qualified investors and oil field services partners structured access to the revenues those operations generate.

## Who We Work With

- **Institutional investors** — hedge funds, family offices, private equity firms, and asset managers
- **Accredited individual investors** — high-net-worth individuals meeting applicable accreditation standards
- **Oil field services companies** — operators and contractors with relevant Venezuelan or Latin American experience

## Getting Started

To begin the onboarding process, visit our [Express Interest](/forms/contact) page. A member of our team will respond within two business days to schedule an introductory call and, if appropriate, send our due diligence package.

We look forward to welcoming our first class of investment partners.
