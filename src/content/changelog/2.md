## Q4 2025 Highlights

- **Production levels** — Active blocks maintained consistent output in line with projections for the quarter. Full production data is available to partners in the investor portal.
- **Capital deployment** — Infrastructure investment in the priority blocks proceeded on schedule. Phase 1 well rehabilitation is complete; Phase 2 workover program commenced in December.
- **Regulatory standing** — All E&P agreements remain in good standing with Venezuelan authorities. No adverse regulatory developments to report.

## Oil Field Services Operations

Our integrated oil field services team completed rehabilitation of three priority wells during the quarter. The workover program has extended productive well life and improved per-well output rates.

Field services partners have been onboarded and are performing in line with contracted service standards.

## What to Expect in Q1 2026

- Completion of Phase 2 workover program across additional wells
- First quarterly investor distributions for production-linked revenue partners
- Updated reserve summary to be provided to institutional investors upon request
- Site visit availability opening for qualified investors who have completed due diligence
