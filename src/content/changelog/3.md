## New Partnerships

DerivaConnect has executed long-term service agreements with two additional oil field services operators with established track records in Venezuelan operations. Both partners bring specialized expertise relevant to our current production profile:

- **Wellbore services** — additional capacity for workover and completion operations across the Orinoco Belt blocks
- **Surface facilities** — expanded capability for processing and transportation infrastructure maintenance

All new service agreements are structured under the same contractual framework as our existing partnerships: defined performance standards, international dispute resolution, and clear operational responsibility allocation.

## Why This Matters for Investors

Integrated oil field services are central to DerivaConnect's investment thesis. By controlling both the E&P rights and the field services operations, we eliminate the margin leakage and operational friction that comes from relying on unaffiliated third-party contractors.

The expansion of our services partner network directly supports production growth targets for 2026 and beyond.

## Updated Capital Deployment Timeline

With expanded operational capacity in place, we are revising our 2026 capital deployment schedule to reflect:

- Accelerated Phase 2 workover completion (target: end of Q2 2026)
- Earlier-than-projected commencement of Phase 3 new well program
- No change to projected distribution timeline for production-linked revenue partners
