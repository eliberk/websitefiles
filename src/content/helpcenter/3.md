## What does DerivaConnect actually do?

DerivaConnect holds contractually secured agreements to conduct oil exploration and production in Venezuela. We combine those E&P rights with fully integrated oil field services operations to produce oil, and we offer qualified investors and oil field services partners structured access to the revenue those operations generate.

In plain terms: we have the legal right to produce oil in Venezuela, we have the operational capability to do it, and we offer investment partners a defined share of what that produces.

## What does "pure play" mean?

"Pure play" means that DerivaConnect's operations are exclusively focused on Venezuelan oil and gas. We are not a diversified energy fund, a commodities trader, or a company with exposure to multiple unrelated assets.

If you invest with DerivaConnect, your exposure is specifically to Venezuelan oil and gas production — nothing more, nothing less.

## What does "contractually secured" mean?

When we say revenues are contractually secured, we mean:

- **E&P rights** are governed by formal legal agreements with Venezuelan authorities, specifying the blocks we operate, the production entitlements we receive, and the term of the agreement
- **Oil field services** are provided under long-term agreements with our service partners, defining operational responsibilities and performance standards
- **Revenue allocation** follows defined contractual formulas — cost recovery first, then profit splits — with no ambiguity about how proceeds are divided
- **Dispute resolution** is handled through international arbitration under ICC or ICSID rules, not Venezuelan domestic courts

This does not mean zero risk. It means that the investment terms are clearly defined in writing and enforceable through established legal mechanisms.

## What is the investment term?

Investment terms vary depending on the specific structure. Production-linked revenue agreements typically have a defined term of three to seven years, with provisions for renewal. Equity JV participation operates on the term of the underlying production license.

## How are returns distributed?

1. Operating costs are recovered from gross production revenues
2. Applicable royalties are paid to the Venezuelan state
3. The remaining net revenue is split according to ownership and contractual entitlements
4. DerivaConnect distributes investor proceeds on a quarterly basis

## What are the minimum investment thresholds?

Minimum thresholds vary by structure and investor type. Contact us directly to discuss the specific threshold applicable to your situation.

Yes. We encourage serious investors to conduct site visits as part of their due diligence. Our operations team can arrange field visits to Venezuelan assets for qualified investors.
