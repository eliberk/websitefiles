## Step 1 — Express Your Interest

The first step is simple: fill out the contact form on our [Express Interest](/forms/contact) page. Provide your name, company (if applicable), email, and a brief note about your investment objectives or the type of partnership you're exploring.

There is no commitment required at this stage. We want to understand who you are and what you're looking for before we send you materials.

## Step 2 — Initial Conversation

Within two business days of receiving your inquiry, a member of the DerivaConnect team will reach out to schedule an introductory call. This conversation typically covers:

- Your investment objectives, timeline, and capital range
- An overview of DerivaConnect's current operations and contract structure
- Whether you are approaching as an individual investor, institutional investor, or oil field services partner
- Any initial questions you have about Venezuela's oil and gas sector

## Step 3 — Due Diligence Package

Qualified investors who wish to proceed receive our standard due diligence package, which includes:

- Overview of DerivaConnect's E&P contract structure and legal framework
- Summary of the operating blocks and reserve estimates
- Description of the oil field services partnerships in place
- Outline of the investment structure, return profile, and risk factors
- References and legal documentation upon request

## Step 4 — Partner Portal Access

Investors who proceed to a formal partnership are given access to the DerivaConnect partner portal, where they can:

- Track production performance and revenue reports
- Access updated legal and compliance documentation
- Communicate directly with the operations team
- Review quarterly investor updates

## Who Is Eligible

DerivaConnect works with:

- **Institutional investors** — hedge funds, family offices, private equity firms, and asset managers
- **Accredited individual investors** — high-net-worth individuals meeting applicable accreditation standards
- **Oil field services companies** — operators and contractors with relevant Venezuelan or Latin American experience

If you are unsure whether you qualify, reach out through the contact form and we will clarify.
