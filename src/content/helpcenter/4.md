## Our Approach to Due Diligence

DerivaConnect believes that well-informed investors make better long-term partners. We actively encourage prospective investors to conduct thorough due diligence before committing capital, and we provide comprehensive documentation to support that process.

If a prospective partner is not asking hard questions, that concerns us more than one who is.

## What We Provide

**Legal documentation**
- Summary of E&P contract terms and the legal entities holding production rights
- Jurisdiction of incorporation and regulatory standing of operating entities
- Overview of applicable dispute resolution provisions
- Regulatory compliance summary for relevant jurisdictions (Venezuela, investor domicile)

**Operational documentation**
- Block location maps and reserve summaries
- Production history and current output data for active blocks
- Description of oil field services partnerships and operator qualifications
- Summary of infrastructure status and capital deployment plan

**Financial documentation**
- Historical revenue and cost structure (for active operations)
- Projected return model with stated assumptions
- Oil price sensitivity analysis
- Cost recovery and profit split calculations

**Third-party verification**
- Reserve certification by qualified independent petroleum engineers (available for institutional investors)
- Legal opinion from qualified Venezuelan counsel
- References from existing investment partners (subject to their consent)

## The Due Diligence Process

**Step 1 — NDA execution**
Full documentation is provided under a mutual non-disclosure agreement. This protects both parties.

**Step 2 — Document review**
We provide our standard due diligence package electronically. Most investors complete initial document review within one to two weeks.

**Step 3 — Q&A sessions**
We schedule dedicated calls with our legal, financial, and operations teams to address specific questions. Complex questions get direct answers from the relevant subject matter expert, not a generalist.

**Step 4 — Site visit (optional but encouraged)**
Qualified investors are invited to visit DerivaConnect's Venezuelan operations. Seeing the assets, meeting the field team, and reviewing on-the-ground conditions is the most effective form of due diligence available.

**Step 5 — Investment committee review**
Most institutional investors will want to complete their own internal investment committee process. We provide any supplemental materials requested to support this review.

## Red Flags We Take Seriously

We want investors to walk away satisfied that they understand the risks. Specific areas we encourage you to probe:

- **Sanctions compliance** — confirm your legal counsel has reviewed the applicable OFAC and EU sanctions framework
- **PDVSA counterparty risk** — understand the specific legal entity that is the counterparty to our production agreements
- **Production verification** — ask for independent production data, not just our reported figures
- **Exit liquidity** — understand how and when you can exit the investment

We will not gloss over these questions. If an answer is uncertain, we will say so.
